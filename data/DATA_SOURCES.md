# Data sources

All three files are CDC surveillance data extracts. All are U.S.
government works and are in the public domain in the United States (17
U.S.C. §105); no permission is needed to reuse or redistribute them, but
please still cite CDC as the originating source (see citations below).

---

## `ARPSP_MDR-Ecoli---Percent-Resistant-in-2024.csv`

- **Source:** CDC Antibiotic Resistance & Patient Safety Portal (ARPSP)
- **Portal:** <https://arpsp.cdc.gov/>
- **Contents:** Percent of multidrug-resistant (MDR) *E. coli* isolates
  among healthcare-associated infections (HAIs), by U.S. state, 2024.
  One row per state/territory.
- **To get a current extract:** go to the ARPSP Antibiotic Resistance
  profile (<https://arpsp.cdc.gov/profile/antibiotic-resistance>),
  select the MDR *E. coli* phenotype, and use the portal's download
  option.

## `BEAM_Dashboard_-_Report_Data_20260804.csv`

- **Source:** CDC BEAM Dashboard (Bacteria, Enterics, Ameba, and
  Mycotics), which combines data from SEDRIC, NARMS (National
  Antimicrobial Resistance Monitoring System for Enteric Bacteria), and
  NORS.
- **Portal:** <https://www.cdc.gov/beam/dashboard/index.html>
- **Contents:** Monthly isolate counts for enteric pathogens
  (*Salmonella*, STEC, *Campylobacter*, *Shigella*, *Vibrio*) by U.S.
  state and source type (Animal, Food, Human, Environment), 2018
  onward, including the percent of isolates with clinically important
  antimicrobial resistance where available.
- **Export date:** August 4, 2026 (encoded in the filename, per the
  dashboard's own export-naming convention).
- **To get a current extract:** use the dashboard's report/download
  feature directly; filters (pathogen, source type, date range, state)
  are selectable on the page before export.

## `2024-SIR-ACH.xlsx`

- **Source:** CDC National Healthcare Safety Network (NHSN),
  Standardized Infection Ratio (SIR) report, Acute Care Hospitals (ACH),
  2024.
- **Portal:** <https://www.cdc.gov/healthcare-associated-infections/php/data/progress-report.html>
- **Contents:** National- and state-level SIRs (observed vs. predicted
  infections) for CLABSI, CAUTI, VAE, MRSA bacteremia, C. difficile
  infection (CDI), and select SSIs, by state, for hospitals reporting
  through NHSN in 2024. Multi-sheet workbook; the exercise uses **Table
  7 (State MRSA Data)**.
- **To get a current extract:** CDC publishes an updated national/state
  HAI progress report annually on the page above; download the most
  recent year's workbook.
